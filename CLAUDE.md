# Celtivo

Product direction and the build plan live in `docs/product/`:

- `direction.md` — what Celtivo is, why the first feature is the email-forwarding trip board,
  principles, deferred ideas, and the decision log.
- `roadmap.md` — phases with scope, done-when criteria and open questions. Work is scoped one
  phase (or part of one) per session.
- `email-ingestion.md` — design notes for the forwarding pipeline: architecture, extraction,
  AI cost, privacy and security.

Before scoping or building a feature, read its phase in the roadmap and the docs it links. When a
decision is made or a phase moves on, update these docs in the same change.

`README.md` covers scripts, the stack, the source layout and the design rules.
